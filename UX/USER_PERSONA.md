# User Personas — Prompt AI Studio (Phase 1)

## 1. Purpose

This document defines representative user personas to guide product and UX decisions. Personas are illustrative and do not represent real individuals or companies.

## 2. Persona 1 — Agency Content Strategist

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name (placeholder) | [Persona A — e.g., "Sara"] |
| Role | Content Strategist at a marketing agency |
| Goals | Manage consistent brand voice across multiple clients; reduce time spent rewriting prompts from scratch |
| Pain Points | Prompts scattered across chats and docs; new team members don't know each client's tone |
| How Prompt AI Studio Helps | Central Brand Brain per client; reusable, categorized Prompt Library |

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|

## 5. Persona 4 — Early-Stage Startup Founder

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name (placeholder) | [Persona D — e.g., "Reza"] |
| Role | Founder/solo marketer at an early-stage startup |
| Goals | Define a clear brand voice early and keep content consistent while the team is small |
| Pain Points | No formal brand guidelines yet; content quality depends on whoever is writing that day |
| How Prompt AI Studio Helps | Guided Brand Brain creation acts as a lightweight brand guideline system |

## 6. Cross-Persona Needs

- All personas value **consistency** over raw content volume.
- All personas want to **reduce repeated explanation** of brand tone to AI tools.
- All personas benefit from a **structured, searchable** system rather than free-form notes.
