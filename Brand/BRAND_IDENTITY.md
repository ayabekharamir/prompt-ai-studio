# Brand Identity — Prompt AI Studio

## 1. Overview

**Brand Name:** Prompt AI Studio
**Persian Brand Name:** استودیو پرامپت هوشمند
**English Tagline:** Build Your Brand's AI Brain
**Persian Tagline:** مغز هوشمند ساخت محتوای برندها

Prompt AI Studio is a professional platform that helps brands build, organize, and manage the intelligence layer behind their content — starting with structured prompt management and evolving into a full AI Brand Agent.

## 2. Brand Essence

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Core Idea | The "brain" that powers a brand's AI-driven content |
| Category | Brand Intelligence & Prompt Management Platform |
| Positioning | The professional layer between a brand's identity and AI content generation |
| Personality | Intelligent, structured, trustworthy, forward-looking |
| Promise | Every brand deserves a consistent, intelligent, and reusable content brain |

## 3. Name Rationale

"Prompt AI Studio" communicates three ideas simultaneously:

- **Prompt** — the fundamental unit of instruction for AI systems.
- **AI** — the technology layer the product is built around.
- **Studio** — a professional, creative, and structured workspace, not a single-purpose tool.

The name signals that this is a workshop for building AI-driven brand assets, not a one-off generator.

## 4. Brand Pillars

1. **Structure** — Prompts and brand knowledge are organized, versioned, and reusable, not scattered across chats and documents.
2. **Intelligence** — The platform captures a brand's tone, values, and positioning as structured, machine-usable knowledge.
3. **Consistency** — Every piece of AI-assisted content should sound like the same brand, every time.
4. **Evolution** — The platform is designed to grow from prompt management into full brand-level AI agents.

## 5. Brand Architecture (Phase Awareness)

Prompt AI Studio is designed as a long-term platform, not a single feature:

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Brand Brain + Prompt Generator |
| Phase 2 | AI Prompt Optimization |
| Phase 3 | AI Content Generation |
| Phase 4 | AI Brand Agent |
| Phase 5 | Enterprise AI Platform |

Phase 1 establishes the foundation: capturing a brand's identity in a structured "Brand Brain" and turning it into high-quality, reusable prompts.

## 6. Persian Summary | خلاصه فارسی

**استودیو پرامپت هوشمند** پلتفرمی حرفه‌ای است که به برندها کمک می‌کند «مغز هوشمند» تولید محتوای خود را بسازند. نسخه اول این محصول یک تولیدکننده محتوای هوش مصنوعی نیست؛ بلکه یک سیستم مدیریت پرامپت و هوش برند است که پایه‌ای برای نسخه‌های آینده (بهینه‌سازی پرامپت، تولید محتوا و در نهایت ایجنت هوشمند برند) خواهد بود. هدف نهایی این است که هر برند بتواند صدای، لحن و ارزش‌های خود را به‌صورت ساختاریافته و قابل استفاده مجدد در اختیار هوش مصنوعی قرار دهد.
