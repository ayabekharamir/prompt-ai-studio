# Business Model — Prompt AI Studio

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the business model foundation for Prompt AI Studio, Phase 1. It defines value proposition, revenue direction, and key business assumptions. Financial figures are placeholders and must be validated with real market data before investor presentation.

## 2. Value Proposition

**English:**
Prompt AI Studio helps brands turn their identity, tone, and content rules into a structured, reusable "Brand Brain" — eliminating repeated explanation to AI tools and ensuring every piece of AI-assisted content stays consistent with the brand.

**Persian | فارسی:**
استودیو پرامپت هوشمند به برندها کمک می‌کند هویت، لحن و قوانین محتوایی خود را به یک «مغز برند» ساختاریافته و قابل‌استفاده مجدد تبدیل کنند؛ این کار نیاز به توضیح مکرر هویت برند به ابزارهای هوش مصنوعی را حذف می‌کند و تضمین می‌کند هر محتوای تولیدشده با کمک هوش مصنوعی، با هویت برند هماهنگ باقی بماند.

## 3. Customer Segments

| Segment | Value Delivered |

## 4. Revenue Model (Directional)

Phase 1 is designed to validate value before monetization scales. Directional revenue model:

| Model Type | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Freemium | Free tier with limited Brand Brains / prompts to drive adoption | Planned |
| Subscription (SaaS) | Monthly/annual plans based on number of workspaces, brands, or prompts | Planned |
| Agency/Team Plans | Higher-tier plans for multi-brand, multi-user agencies | Planned |
| Future: Usage-Based AI Features | In Phases 2–3, usage-based pricing may apply to AI optimization/generation calls | Future consideration |

Detailed tiers and prices are defined in `Business/PRICING.md`.
