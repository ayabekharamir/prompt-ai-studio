# Pricing Strategy — Prompt AI Studio

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines a directional pricing structure for Phase 1. All prices are placeholders (`[TBD]`) pending market validation and are not final commercial commitments.

## 2. Pricing Philosophy

- Pricing should reflect **value delivered through consistency and reuse**, not raw AI compute cost (Phase 1 has no generation cost, since it does not call AI models for content generation).
- Entry-level access should be low-friction to encourage adoption and feedback during the validation stage.
- Pricing should scale naturally with the number of brands/workspaces managed — a clear proxy for value to agencies and multi-brand users.

## 4. Pricing Considerations by Phase

| Phase | Pricing Implication |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Pricing based on Brand Brain/workspace limits (no AI usage cost) |
| Phase 2 | Potential add-on pricing for AI-assisted prompt optimization |
| Phase 3 | Usage-based component likely required for AI content generation (model API costs) |
| Phase 4–5 | Enterprise and agent-based pricing models to be developed |

## 5. What Is Explicitly Not Defined Yet

- Exact price points per tier.
- Annual discount structure.
- Currency/localization strategy (e.g., Rial-based pricing for the Iranian market).
- Free-tier usage limits (number of prompts, Brand Brains).

These require real market testing and are intentionally left open in Phase 1.
