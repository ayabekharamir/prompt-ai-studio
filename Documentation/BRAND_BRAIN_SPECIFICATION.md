# Prompt AI Studio
# Brand Brain Specification


## 1. Concept


Brand Brain is the core intelligence layer of Prompt AI Studio.

It stores everything an AI system needs to understand a brand.

The goal:

Every generated prompt should feel like it was created specifically for that brand.



---

# 2. Brand Brain Structure


Brand Brain consists of:


1. Brand Identity

2. Brand Voice

3. Audience Intelligence

4. Visual Intelligence

5. Content Intelligence

6. Business Intelligence

7. Brand Rules



---

# 3. Brand Identity


Purpose:

Define who the brand is.


Information:


Brand Name

Industry

Mission

Vision

Values

History

Positioning

Unique Selling Proposition (USP)



Example:


Brand:

IranCamp


Industry:

Travel & Adventure


Positioning:

Premium nature experiences



---

# 4. Brand Voice


Purpose:

Define how the brand communicates.


Information:


Personality:


Examples:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Luxury
- Energetic
- Educational



Tone:


Examples:

- Formal
- Casual
- Emotional
- Inspirational



Communication Rules:


Should say:

- Clear
- Positive
- Human


Should avoid:

- Aggressive language
- Fake promises



---

# 5. Audience Intelligence


Purpose:

Understand customers.


Information:


Target Audience:

Age

Location

Interests

Problems

Needs

Motivations

Buying Behavior



Customer Persona:


Name

Background

Goals

Pain Points



---

# 6. Visual Intelligence


Purpose:

Maintain visual consistency.


Information:


Color Palette

Typography

Photography Style

Illustration Style

Design References

Image Rules



Examples:


Preferred:

Natural lighting

Premium photography

Clean composition


Avoid:

Low quality images

Random styles



---

# 7. Content Intelligence


Purpose:

Define content strategy.


Information:


Main Content Types:


- Social Media
- Blog
- Advertisement
- Video
- Email



Content Goals:


- Awareness
- Engagement
- Conversion
- Education



Content Structure:


Hook

Value

Call To Action



---

# 8. Business Intelligence


Purpose:

Understand business objectives.


Information:


Products

Services

Pricing Position

Market

Competitors

Goals



---

# 9. Brand Rules


Purpose:

Protect brand consistency.


Rules:


Must Include:

- Brand tone
- Key messages
- Values


Must Avoid:

- Forbidden words
- Wrong claims
- Inconsistent messaging



---

# 10. Prompt Generation Flow


User creates Brand Brain


↓

System analyzes brand information


↓

Brand Context is created


↓

Prompt Template selected


↓

Brand-specific Prompt generated



---

# 11. Future AI Integration


Future AI models will receive:


Brand Context

+

User Goal

+

Prompt Template


Output:


Optimized AI Prompt



---

# 12. Data Ownership


Brand data belongs to the user.

Principles:


- Privacy first
- User control
- Export capability
- Secure storage
